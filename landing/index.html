What was EasyFocus?

Banking, rebuilt around accessibility.

A familiar mobile-banking app — with an AI assistant on the surface and a real microservices platform underneath.

Banking apps overwhelm people who think differently. EasyFocus remembers, watches, explains and adapts — so you don't have to.

Microservices Kubernetes GitOps · Argo CD Kafka RAG chatbot Observability Terraform · GKE
